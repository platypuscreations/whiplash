Whiplash Player plays music with classic Winamp 2.x skins, on macOS and Linux.

**Download** the file for your computer from **Assets** below:

- **macOS** (Apple silicon, macOS 11 or later): the `.dmg`
- **Debian, Ubuntu and similar** (64-bit PC): the `.deb`
- **Other Linux** (64-bit PC): the `.AppImage`

Whiplash comes with its own skin and plays classic Winamp skins (`.wsz`) too. macOS asks you to approve it the first time it opens. The [README](https://github.com/platypuscreations/whiplash#install) walks through installing it and getting more skins, such as the classic base 2.91.
